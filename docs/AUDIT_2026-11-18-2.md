## New findings: two PIN entry points the N19 rule missed

The approver-PIN rule (N19) was enforced in the staff screen and at every place a PIN is
used. A sweep of every place a PIN is *created* found two more.

### N20 — Low: setup accepted a 4-digit owner PIN

`npm run db:bootstrap` accepted `BOOTSTRAP_PIN` of 4–8 digits, and the deployment guide's
example was `4567`. A new installation's owner would get a PIN that every approval then
refuses.

**Fixed in PR #37:**

- the bootstrap requires 6–8 digits;
- both deployment guides show a 6-digit example.

Verified by running the bootstrap: `4567` is refused with the new message; `456789` passes
validation.

### N21 — Low: the legacy import gave managers 4-digit PINs

`scripts/import-restpos.ts` gave every imported account a random 4-digit PIN, including
the owner and managers, whose PINs approve. Their PINs would have been refused from the
first day. The import test asserted exactly that ("fresh 4-digit PIN" for the admin).

**Fixed in PR #37:**

- approver roles get a random 6-digit PIN and other staff keep 4 digits;
- the test now requires 6 digits for the imported owner and passes;
- `docs/LEGACY_IMPORT.md` is updated.
