### Review of PR #35

- `GET /api/audit-logs` keeps its `canViewTransactionTrail` gate.
- `GET /api/inventory/counts` and the new `GET /api/inventory/movements` are gated by
  `canModifyInventory` through `secureRoute`.
- All three go through `src/lib/audit-list-query.ts`:
  - `limit` is capped at 100 or 500;
  - `offset` must be a non-negative integer;
  - `from`/`to` must be given together and are validated by `reportWindow` (strict dates, no reversed range).
- Filtering happens before paging, and `hasMore` uses a `limit + 1` probe.
- The routes no longer return raw `err.message` to the browser. RLS keeps the stock-count
  join to `users` inside the branch.
- New tests in `tests/security.ts` cover the endpoints.

No new findings.

## Recommendations

The first audit's recommendations stand unchanged; nothing in them has been started.
In order:

1. **P2: login lockout.** Small. Anyone online can block all sign-ins.
2. **N5: owner PIN discovery.** Small. Keep the owner out of quick PIN switch; stop revealing PIN collisions.
3. **N3 + N4: item removal and voids.** Medium. Additions-only for till staff, a manager PIN for removals and cancels, and the actor recorded on every audit row (this also covers most of N7).
4. **P1: automated off-site backups.** Small to medium. A shipped script and timer, an encrypted off-site copy and a rehearsed restore.
5. **First month:** P5 (database timeouts), P4 (session renewal), P6 (async password hashing), N6 (permission-based approvals), N7 (remaining audit rows), P8 (uptime and backup alerts), P3 (release-directory deploys).
6. **N2** whenever the owner is ready: make the repo private, purge the history, and rotate legacy credentials in the meantime.

Each fix should be its own PR that cites the finding ID, adds a test, and updates its row
in `FINDINGS.md`, per [`AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md).
